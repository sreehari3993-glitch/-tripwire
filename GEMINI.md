# Project Guidelines & Rules

## UI & Animation Preservation Rule
**CRITICAL RULE FOR ALL AI AGENTS:**
- **DO NOT IMPROVE OR MODIFY THE UI OR ANIMATIONS**: The UI and animations must stand as they are now.
- Do not redesign layouts, colors, CSS, component styling, or visual flourishes.
- Do not add or tweak animations, transitions, or effects.
- All development and assistant focus must remain on backend APIs, business logic, data models, and backend/frontend functional wiring without altering the visual appearance.
